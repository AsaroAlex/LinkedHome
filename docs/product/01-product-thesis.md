# Product thesis — Phase 5

Selected product brand: **LinkedHome**, a private local MVP of an Italy-first reverse rental marketplace. “Find a relevant conversation while keeping control of what you share.” This is a testable proposition, not a promise of a home, solvency or discrimination-free selection.

The user has approved the brand; trademark clearance remains open, including nearby Linkhome and Linkedhomes names. The proposed future main domain is `linkedhome.eu`, with `.it` optional; `linkedhome.com` is already registered. No domain has been purchased or real origin/SMTP sender changed. The [domain plan](../operations/domain-setup.md) records the next steps. Earlier naming and validation evidence remains historical.

The tenant prepares structured search preferences and deliberately publishes them. A landlord with a current property sees compatible pseudonymous profiles and why the declared criteria fit. The landlord sends a property-specific invitation; the tenant accepts or declines; acceptance opens a private conversation. Either party can close, block or report. Pausing a profile stops discovery and new invitations and cancels pending invitations; existing accepted conversations remain explicitly accessible until closed. Pausing a property stops discovery/invitations and cancels pending invitations; closing a conversation prevents further messages.

The first useful outcome is an accepted relevant invitation, not a completed dossier, verification badge or registration. Tenants never pay for visibility/contact. No billing, commissions, deposits, guarantee, negotiation or government/provider identity checks in this version. No ML, global person score, desirability rank, salary/employer/nationality filters or private document upload. Unknown optional verification never suppresses discovery.

Bologna is a synthetic example catchment, not an approved launch. The initial population is adults seeking residential rentals and people authorised to offer one, including users with both roles. Admin/moderator privileges can only be provisioned by an operator, never selected at signup. Real users and commercial launch require the release checklist; the local environment is intended for generated examples only.

Evidence: research 01/08, primary recheck and review02. Reusable profiles and reverse contact are already offered by competitors; defensibility and user preference are unproven. The scarce-side hypothesis requires voluntary repeat participation by landlords with current vacancies. We will stop or change direction if contact browsing adds work, useful invitations stay rare, or disclosure control is misunderstood.

Success measurement follows fixed publication cohorts (research08): opportunity within14 days, supply conversation within7, acceptance within72 hours, time to event with no-event observations, withdrawals, safety/control outcomes and staff cost. Local demo statistics report actual stored workflow counts, not market traction or validated KPI targets. No advertising trackers or inferred protected characteristics.

Research experiments remain proposed. Recruitment, notices, handling, decision thresholds and a named operator must be approved before external studies. Internal testing can establish behaviour and access control, not product-market fit.
