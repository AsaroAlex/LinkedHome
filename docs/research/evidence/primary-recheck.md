Source: [Idealista, 20 January 2026](https://www.idealista.it/news/immobiliare/residenziale/2026/01/20/312325-case-in-vendita-e-in-affitto-i-capoluoghi-piu-richiesti-a-fine-2025), read 3 October. ## R-GDPR

Source: Garante-hosted official Italian annotated GDPR PDF, exact URL/hash in manifest. PDF title includes rectifications of 23 May 2018; this is a verified baseline text, not a claim to have checked all subsequent legal developments. Read Arts. 5, 6, 9, 12, 22, 25, 32, 35. Art. 5 requires minimisation, purpose and storage limitation, accuracy and security; Art. 6 requires a lawful basis; Art. 9 adds conditions for special categories. Art. 12 covers clear rights communication and response timing. Art. 22 concerns solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects and specified exceptions/safeguards. Arts. 25/32 require appropriate design/default/security measures; Art. 35 requires assessment before likely high-risk processing. A human click or deterministic algorithm is not a blanket exemption. No legal clearance is inferred.

## Access limits and decision impact

EUR-Lex GDPR/AI Act/DSA/SCHUFA retries returned HTTP 202 with zero bytes and a WAF challenge; they were not read. An official Garante-hosted GDPR alternative was read without bypassing a challenge. An initial Normattiva export without a session was an error page; only the subsequent substantive session export counts. OMI PDF returned site access-denied; InquilinoGiusto/Facile and Bologna institutional sites remained proxy-blocked. Therefore their metrics, novelty comparisons and partnership access are not adopted premises. No signups, interviews, payments or private-provider workflows occurred.

The research supports conservative internal product design with synthetic data. Launch city, moat, pricing, provider availability, processing roles, applicable current legal requirements and operational readiness require later evidence. Removing those unsupported premises is part of closing the desk-research gate, not declaring them true.
