# Review 01 — Research gate

**Date:** 2026-10-02. **Scope:** dossiers 01–10, their source trail, cross-document consistency and the evidence needed before product decisions. **Outcome: BLOCKED for gate approval; document consolidation and internal corrections completed.**

**Review method:** one agent performed eight explicit role-based passes following [the review protocol](README.md). These are A–H perspectives, not eight independent people or agent runs. No customer interviews, qualified legal opinion or independent peer sign-off is implied. The underlying evidence limitations are recorded in [09](../research/09-sources.md).

## A — Founder / strategy

**CRITICAL ISSUES**

- **R01-C2, OPEN:** the city recommendation and competitive differentiation depend on search excerpts, subjective scores and current product claims whose primary pages were not retrieved. A correct weighted sum does not validate supplier access or market demand. Do not approve the launch city or claim product-market fit from the matrix.

**IMPORTANT ISSUES**

- A portable dossier can be copied and may be rejected by landlords. Reversed contact can add effort on the scarce side. Research O1/O2 before making them the company thesis.
- No market-sizing model or acquisition evidence supports revenue forecasts. A high national stock count does not establish an addressable, willing supply pool.

**NICE-TO-HAVE IMPROVEMENTS**

- Later maintain a compact comparison of competing explanations for low conversion: unavailable property, price, trust, documentation or response effort.

## B — Product

**CRITICAL ISSUES**

- **R01-C3, RESOLVED DOCUMENTALLY:** the feature benchmark marked expensive identity, biometrics and open banking as MUST while the proposed core was meant to remain accessible. Tenant activation required verified income. Candidate/optional status and publication-based activation now replace those requirements; see 04, 06 and 08.
- **R01-C4, RESOLVED DOCUMENTALLY:** result-only sharing still recommended a score/tier and later ML. Recommendations now distinguish scoped evidence from a person-worth score and preserve the no-ML matching constraint.

**IMPORTANT ISSUES**

- Every verification badge needs a precise scope, expiry and dispute meaning; unknown is not failed. A phone/email confirmation cannot be marketed as identity or property verification.
- The research identifies opportunities but deliberately leaves the Phase 5 thesis and Phase 6 scope undecided.

**NICE-TO-HAVE IMPROVEMENTS**

- Explore whether a plain, understandable self-declared profile has enough value before adding provider integrations.

## C — UX

**CRITICAL ISSUES**

- **R01-C5, RESOLVED DOCUMENTALLY:** the old legal draft described a proxy-free discovery card while allowing first names, employment categories and child/adult counts, and alternated default visibility on/off. The revised stage model defaults to unpublished, uses an opaque identifier, excludes household-type filtering and requires deliberate sharing.

**IMPORTANT ISSUES**

- Test unverified, foreign-income, self-employed, pension/benefit, disability/accessibility and low-digital-confidence cases without collecting unnecessary sensitive data.
- "Revoke share" cannot erase a downloaded copy. Tenant acceptance of a match must not be mistaken for release of all documents.
- Italian-first with English prepared controls over the English-first sentence previously in 05.

**NICE-TO-HAVE IMPROVEMENTS**

- Test wording for a failed versus expired versus disputed check using synthetic examples.

## D — Engineering

**CRITICAL ISSUES**

- **R01-C6, RESOLVED DOCUMENTALLY:** PROJECT_STATE described another machine, a still-running researcher and services that were not established here. Current state records `/workspace/LinkedHome`, actual runtime observations and the absence of application code. Old environmental history is not installation evidence.

**IMPORTANT ISSUES**

- Six successful npm metadata checks are not an integrated-stack test. No dependency manifest, lockfile, build or test runner exists yet. Pinning and compatibility belong to the later ADR/scaffolding gate.
- The original city scratchpad was absent. All 17 scores and four alternative weight scenarios were recomputed from the documented table and reproduced in 01, without claiming the missing script ran.

**NICE-TO-HAVE IMPROVEMENTS**

- If the matrix becomes a decision tool, retain structured score inputs and a small reproducible calculation alongside future revisions.

## E — Security / privacy

**CRITICAL ISSUES**

- **R01-C7, RESOLVED DOCUMENTALLY:** the partial legal dossier treated blanket five-year logs, fixed vendor TTLs, "EU hosting" and vendor-as-processor assumptions as settled. Section 07 now separates proposed minimisation from the purpose, role, retention, transfer and provider decisions still required.
- **R01-C1, OPEN:** the applicable primary privacy texts and current guidance could not be retrieved. Legal requirements and deployment-specific conclusions must not be certified from this dossier.

**IMPORTANT ISSUES**

- No raw document, biometric or bank-data workflow is approved. Provider and staff access, incident response, deletion and restores need a later DPIA and implementation validation.
- Protected-group outcome monitoring needs a lawful protocol; fairness is not a blanket justification for collecting sensitive demographics.

**NICE-TO-HAVE IMPROVEMENTS**

- Maintain a threat model as soon as the data flows are designed, including scraping, impersonation, stalking and staff misuse.

## F — Legal / fairness

**CRITICAL ISSUES**

- **R01-C1, OPEN:** legal text retrieval/current amendments remain unverified. The rewritten dossier provides 21 explicit official reading targets, with blocked/unretrieved status; it is not counsel clearance.
- **R01-C8, RESOLVED DOCUMENTALLY:** "flat fee = mediation-safe" and "boolean signals = outside Art. 22" were not defensible categorical conclusions. They were removed from controlling recommendations. Activity, effect, purpose and actual human involvement must be assessed. Success/per-match recommendations in 05 were reconciled with their exclusion in 06.

**IMPORTANT ISSUES**

- Appoint qualified Italian counsel for mediation, payments/insurance if ever added, controller roles and platform/consumer duties before real-data operation. Free access and manual curation do not automatically remove regulation.
- No claim that every private landlord can or cannot obtain every form of credit information is adopted; assess the specific lawful and contractual route.

**NICE-TO-HAVE IMPROVEMENTS**

- Maintain a versioned question pack with actual service screens and data flows for counsel once design is authorised.

## G — Growth / marketplace

**CRITICAL ISSUES**

- **R01-C9, RESOLVED DOCUMENTALLY:** the inherited Milan-first sequence conflicted with the Bologna matrix, and an Airbnb 300/100 analogy was used as a launch threshold. Bologna is now a research-location hypothesis; no transferable threshold or second-city decision is adopted.

**IMPORTANT ISSUES**

- No association, university or municipality is a committed acquisition partner. Listing them is not evidence of low CAC. Direct outreach has not been performed.
- Measure mature search/vacancy cohorts, distinguish invitations from leases, report censoring and early withdrawals. The corrected definitions are in 08 §4; targets remain unset.
- Small landlords may transact infrequently. Verification margin alone cannot justify LTV/CAC assumptions; include support, rechecks, refunds and free usage.

**NICE-TO-HAVE IMPROVEMENTS**

- Investigate different seasonal windows and acquisition routes before deciding when to recruit a pilot cohort.

## H — Adversarial competitor

**CRITICAL ISSUES**

- **R01-C10, RESOLVED DOCUMENTALLY:** the legal draft lacked its bibliography, source IDs collided across dossiers, and unverifiable facts were carried into decision language. Section 09 now namespaces 566 records; 07 uses an explicit replacement bibliography; 01 distinguishes inherited claims from fresh observations. No independent corroboration is inferred from duplicate citations.

**IMPORTANT ISSUES**

- A portal can add a profile and landlord invitation flow. Defensibility must be demonstrated through sustained local supply, accepted evidence and better outcomes, not claims of novelty or regulatory superiority.
- MyTenant/Want2Rent remain unresolved identities. Search failure cannot be used to assert an uncontested category.
- A single author cannot supply eight independent reviews by writing eight headings. Obtain the intended independent challenge before recording a final gate pass.

**NICE-TO-HAVE IMPROVEMENTS**

- Test the strongest substitute—existing agency/portal/message workflows—not only small reverse-marketplace competitors.

**Decision:** retain the research gate as **BLOCKED**. This review completes the current single-agent A–H assessment and document corrections; it does not authorise product/brand/code phases.
